Changelog

Everything that makes Buadren AC different from retail Asheron's Call. Search it, filter it by topic, or link straight to an entry.

The rules right now

  • 5 concurrent clients allowed per account
  • 10x XP and 10x Luminance, each halved (roughly) with every Enlightenment
  • Tier 8 armor can roll either up to +2 D/DR or up to +5 CD/CDR
  • Ratings can roll up to +5 V/HB on jewelry, and +5 D/DR on underclothes and cloaks
  • No spell component burn
  • Fellowships up to 15 — join from anywhere by messaging a member "xp," and any level shares XP evenly with any other

Nov 2026 Link to Catching up: changes we hadn't written down

Catching up: changes we hadn't written down

  • Loot
  • Progression
  • World & quests

Going through the server code turned up a few long-standing changes that never made it into this log. None of these are new; they're just recorded now.

  • Tier 8 clothing, cloaks, and jewelry roll a rating more often: 40% of the time, up from 25%.
  • Tier 8 armor rolls either D/DR (up to +2) or CD/CDR (up to +5, up from +2). About 28% of pieces get one.
  • The Luminance item mana usage augmentation is far stronger: each one counts for 40 rating points instead of 5.
  • Society armor can now be tailored.
  • Quest timers longer than 20 hours are capped at 20 hours.
  • Keylux, in Marketplace and several other locations, trades Ultimate Legendary Keys for a Durable Ultimate Legendary Key with 10 uses (level 150+).
  • Frederick the Forgetful in Marketplace offers items in exchange for DN Remarks, which come from bosses around Dereth.

Shadows of the Realm

  • World & quests
  • Progression

A new daily quest. Archivist Valdus in the Holtburg Inn wants proof that someone is still pushing back the darkness: one trophy from each of five areas, handed to Keeper Serath nearby.

The reward is 50,000 Luminance, once a day. Trophies are a 2% drop from creatures in those areas, and they can be traded.

Mar 2025 Link to Re-balancing the Force: loot enchantment rework

Re-balancing the Force: loot enchantment rework

  • Loot

A buffs-only rebalance (cantrips unaffected):

  • Armor now defaults to 1 enchantment with a chance at a 2nd (previously 0 with a chance at 1). Clothing "armors" are unaffected and remain 1–3 enchantments.
  • Creature, Void, War, and Assess spells removed from spell tables entirely.
  • Tinker skills removed from all tables except Crowns and Tinker set items.
  • Alchemy and Cooking removed from all tables except Leather Caps and Crafter set items.
  • Spell groupings attached to armor slots were reworked across the board.

Nov 2024 Link to Thanksgiving loot update

Thanksgiving loot update

  • Loot
  • Vendors & keys

Aged legendary keys and society trade tokens can now be exchanged for Ultimate Legendary Keys, which open the Ultimate Legendary Key Chests in Arwic.

  • Buy Ultimate Legendary Keys from Keylore in Arwic, or from your Society Quartermaster using society trade tokens (under the Keys tab).
  • Regular legendary chests roll at a 30% luck factor; Ultimate chests roll at 80% — still RNG, but far more likely to favor you.

This exists because aged keys eat pack space and regular legendary chests are slow to pull from — and it gives a reason to run T8 areas that only drop aged keys.

Nov 16, 2024 Link to The Enlightenment update

The Enlightenment update

  • Progression

A redesign of what happens once you enlighten — the requirements to enlighten are unchanged. Maximum Enlightenments raised to 4. Each one grants:

  • 5 skill points to all trained skills
  • 10 vitality points
  • A 50% reduction in XP and Luminance gain from kills
  • 1 Token of Wisdom, spendable with Wendi the Wise in Arwic (who carries rare jewelry, armor, weapons, and other eternal items)

Rare jewelry and armor cantrips were uplifted to Legendary, and rare jewelry gained Essence Glutton. Wendi's rare jewelry, armor, and weapons are attuned/bonded; her Eternal Kits and Mana Charges are bonded.

The XP/Luminance reduction stacks multiplicatively rather than simply subtracting 50% each time:

  • After 1st Enlightenment — 5x rate
  • After 2nd — 2.5x
  • After 3rd — 1.25x
  • After 4th — 0.63x
Do yourself a favor: handle burden augmentations and mule off heavy equipment before committing to Enlightenment. Attributes can reset and leave you well over 300 burden before your Token of Wisdom and Certificate of Attribute Reset arrive. You've been warned.
